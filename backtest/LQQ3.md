## Data and assumptions

- LQQ3 prices: Yahoo Finance daily bars for LQQ3.L (approved for this one series). Isolated bad prints are removed (24 Oct 2025: a flat bar at +39% with no volume).
- Signal: QQQ adjusted close from Alpaca. S&P 500: SPY total return from Alpaca, converted to pounds with FXB (pound sterling trust) as the exchange rate. A sterling S&P 500 fund (VUSA, CSP1) loses about 0.2% a year more to US dividend withholding.
- Cash when out of the market: what FXB's pound deposits paid after its fee (0% until 2022, then about 2.5-3% a year). A money-market fund in an ISA paid more since 2023, so this errs low.
- Not included: platform dealing fees (a few pounds a trade, about 4 trades a year). LQQ3's own costs are already in its price.
- 3x leverage: drawdowns of 60% and more are part of this rule. A crash faster than the 200-day signal (the Nasdaq-100 fell 30% before crossing it in 2000) would cost about two-thirds of the position.
