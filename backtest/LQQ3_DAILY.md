## Caveats

- LQQ3 is the only 3x fund tested, because it is the only London price series approved for this repository. The US multi-fund result (backtest/DAILY.md) relied on nine funds; most have no London equivalent with a long history (WisdomTree's S&P 500 3x, 3LUS / 3USL, does).
- 104 settings were tried; the whole-period winners are flattered by selection. The pick was chosen on 2017-21 only, so its 2022-26 column is the honest test.
- The London open of a 3x fund can have a wide spread; the close (16:30 UK, with the US market open) is usually tighter. Platform commissions come on top; inside an ISA there is no tax on the gains.
- A 3x fund can lose most of its value in a crash; nothing can be sold before the next London session after a US-close signal.
