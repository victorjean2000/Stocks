## Caveats

- Ten years is short and mostly a bull market led by large US tech. The Nasdaq-100 itself beat SPY by about 5% a year, which lifts every tech-heavy strategy. Momentum crashes (2009) and a dot-com style bear market are not in the sample.
- Leveraged ETFs reset daily. A fast crash hits them before a 200-day filter can react: when the Nasdaq-100 fell 30% before crossing its 200-day average in 2000, a 3x fund would have lost about two-thirds.
- Stock strategies use today's listed stocks only (see Survivorship). No market-impact model; taxes ignored (monthly turnover creates short-term gains).
- Many settings were tried; the best of many backtests is always flattered by luck. Prefer families that work across most of their grid over the single best row.
