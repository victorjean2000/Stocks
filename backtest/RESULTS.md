## Caveats

- Survivorship bias: only stocks listed today are in the data; companies that were acquired, went bankrupt or were delisted are missing, which flatters results (most for momentum). Compare with the equal-weight universe, which has the same bias, rather than with SPY alone.
- Fills at the next open with fixed costs; no market-impact model, no borrow, cash earns 0% (this understates the market-filter variants, which sit in cash for long spells).
- The earnings blackout uses the actual release dates, i.e. assumes each date is known at least 6 sessions ahead. Companies usually announce it 2 to 4 weeks before, but not always.
- Sector is today's SIC-based sector for every date.
