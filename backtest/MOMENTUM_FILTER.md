## Notes

- Only 2017-26 can be tested (the point-in-time stock data starts in 2016): one long bear market (2022) and several fast falls. A filter earns its keep in long bear markets like 2000-02 and 2008, which are not in the sample; in fast falls it sells near the bottom and buys back higher.
- Daily filters trade the whole portfolio at each switch (costs included); in an ISA or a pension there is no tax on that, in a taxable account there would be.
- Cash earns T-bill interest (BIL) while the filter is out.
